\section{Research Translation}
\begin{content}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item MAP Protocol (Singapore, \url{https://www.mapprotocol.io}): a full-chain interoperability protocol based on light clients and secure multi-party computation (MPC), designed for trustless peer-to-peer exchange between Bitcoin (BTC) and multi-chain stablecoins and tokenized assets. The protocol has processed cross-chain asset transfers at the million-level per day, accumulated transaction fees exceeding 100 million, and successfully launched its token.
        \item PolyLink (\url{https://polylink.evan.cafe}): a blockchain-based open platform for decentralized edge AI computing. It aggregates idle GPU resources worldwide through the PolyCoin incentive mechanism and uses intelligent scheduling to support distributed inference for large language models (LLMs). The platform has connected high-end GPU resources from multiple universities worldwide.
        \item Derivation (Hong Kong, \url{https://www.derivation.dev}): a technology company focused on blockchain, providing customized technical solutions grounded in mathematics and cryptography, and having supported multiple blockchain projects.
        \item SynFutures (Hong Kong, \url{https://www.synfutures.com}): a decentralized platform for perpetual and futures trading across arbitrary assets. It adopts the Oyster AMM mechanism to provide liquidity and stable quotations, with daily trading volume exceeding one million U.S. dollars.
    \end{enumerate}
